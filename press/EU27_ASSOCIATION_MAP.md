## Permission-based rollout
1. Confirm the current legal association, website and official editorial/partnership process using its latest own publications.
2. Ask whether it permits voluntary external features, co-hosted synthetic-data demos or a no-fee article/banner **before** placing materials.
3. Check country-specific ePrivacy/GDPR rules, recipient role, any applicable opt-in or exception, objections, suppression and sender legitimacy. Membership/public email is not general marketing consent.
4. Obtain actual permission from an authorized editor. Do not scrape member names, guest data, personal contact details or any third-party property records.
5. Record independent evidence to move each organization from `RESEARCHED` to `REQUEST_ALLOWED`, `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED`, `PUBLISHED`. Do not mark an organization as `PARTNER_CONTRACTED` without executed terms.
6. Any commercial co-marketing/referral fees or revenue share require a separate signed contract, disclosure and tax review; they are not active here.
