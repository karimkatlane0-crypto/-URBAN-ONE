# URBAN ONE — European hotel, B&B & hospitality association cooperation
**Public, non-binding proposal · 22 October 2026**

## Proposed win–win for associations
We invite **hotel federations, local hospitality associations, bed-and-breakfast networks, tourism stakeholders, local enterprise alliances and sector publications** to evaluate a free introductory editorial mention or a member webinar/demo for the independent URBAN ONE software project. The association is free to decline and retains editorial control.

### What members could receive
1. An **introductory overview or synthetic-data demonstration**, with no claim of live PMS, banking or provider integration.
2. A scoped written assessment for guest journey/check-in/out operations, optional-service catalogues, data/process governance and service workflows.
3. Optional paid B2B software/process work **only after a separately negotiated agreement** stating deliverables, support, seller/merchant, EUR price, taxes, withdrawal/refund conditions and lawful data handling.
4. Future provider-dependent possibilities — Booking/PMS mapping, AIS/PIS feasibility, contract e-signature — available only after verified partner approvals and tenant/property consent.

### Voluntary no-cost editorial cooperation
- Association/blog **may choose** to publish a short summary or original URBAN ONE creative asset after editorial review.
- URBAN ONE supplies a press kit and a secure route for written enquiries.
- No obligation to endorse URBAN ONE, place the badge, share members' personal data, buy subscriptions, grant access to properties or guarantee traffic.
- No use of any association's official badge/logo without its written licence.
- This is an **invitation to consider cooperation**, not an existing convention, membership benefit or concluded contract.

### Optional revenue collaboration — only by separate contract
If both sides later agree, lawful models to assess include an explicitly disclosed referral fee for a completed, verifiable new B2B service contract, co-hosted training delivery, a negotiated SaaS volume price or a tailored enterprise licence. **No 18% commission or automatic revenue share is payable to associations by default.** A proposed URBAN ONE gross business share in other internal models is not a personal or third-party entitlement.

Referral programme controls: legal eligibility for the country, right to make commercial introductions, affiliate/advertising disclosure, written terms, eligible event definition, anti-fraud and deduplication, refunds, VAT, payment-service-provider requirements and approved payout recipient. No payment or transfer is authorized by this file.

### Provider and hospitality boundaries
- No assertion of a Booking.com connectivity partnership or OTA accommodation payment entitlement.
- No assertion of Fabrick production AIS/PIS, InfoCert business-partner contract, real qualified signatures or bank access.
- Gintitic is a credit-workflow concept with supervised legal review, **not** a licensed debt-collection undertaking or established recovery mandate.
- No claim of all European real properties indexed or rights to owners' personal data.
- No affiliation or revenue split with OpenAI/ChatGPT is implied.
- No guaranteed wealth, sales, media coverage, lead volume, hotel acquisition or bookings.
