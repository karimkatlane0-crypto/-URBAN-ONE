Before an **individual** outreach message, obtain the current editorial submission instructions and verify whether the recipient is an authorized press/editorial contact versus a general business contact; obtain jurisdiction-specific advice as required. A public email address alone does not prove lawful unsolicited B2B marketing. Do not scrape member lists or use association membership as marketing permission.
