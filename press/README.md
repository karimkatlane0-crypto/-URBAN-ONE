1. Third-party associations own their editorial spaces and must expressly accept banner or story placement; an organisation in a directory is **not** a partner.
2. Do not send bulk cold marketing, unsolicited SMS or scraped contact lists. Check national ePrivacy rules, GDPR, suppression/objections and the lawful role of the recipient before targeted requests.
3. Ordinary editorial pickup is voluntary and unpaid. Paid sponsored coverage, any affiliate commission, referral share or revenue split requires advance written agreement and any legally required commercial disclosure.
4. Never link a banner to a payment page as though it were a confirmed association discount or member service. Link first to an accurate product page or approved contact route.
5. Price, merchant identity, tax, privacy notice, fulfilment, cancellation and provider limitations must be verified before any actual sale.
