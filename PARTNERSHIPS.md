# URBAN ONE — Global Partnership & Developer Call

URBAN ONE is looking for developers, technical reviewers and strategic partners interested in building and validating an auditable, consent-aware control plane spanning AI-enabled workflows, Open Banking concepts, data orchestration, local-first mobile/web access and governed privileged actions.

## Who we want to meet

- backend, API gateway and integration engineers
- AI agent, LLM, inference and orchestration developers
- DevSecOps, cloud security, IAM and zero-trust specialists
- data engineering, MLOps and observability teams
- Android, iOS, Capacitor, PWA and local-first developers
- MCP, workflow automation and enterprise integration specialists

### Companies and strategic partners
- banks and regulated financial institutions
- Open Banking infrastructure providers
- payment service providers and identity providers
- fintech and regtech companies
- cybersecurity and compliance technology companies
- cloud, AI and data-platform companies

- travel, booking, commerce and digital-service operators
- research institutions and standards-focused engineering groups

## Collaboration opportunities

1. **Technical due diligence** — architecture, threat model, RBAC, consent, provenance and evidence review.
2. **Provider integration** — connect approved APIs and licensed providers behind explicit scopes and human approval gates.
3. **Security engineering** — testing, hardening, secrets management, IAM, SCA and auditability.
4. **Open Banking pilots** — proof-of-concept work using compliant sandbox or licensed provider environments.
5. **AI & data integration** — governed model-provider routing, redaction, provenance and observability.
6. **Mobile release engineering** — Android/iOS build, signing, device tests and controlled distribution.
7. **Commercial partnerships** — licensing, pilots, joint integrations and enterprise deployment evaluation.

## Engineering principles
- deny-by-default authorization
- explicit scopes and consent
- human approval for privileged financial actions
- auditable receipts and evidence
- no secrets in client bundles
- data minimization and redaction before model handoff
- clear separation between prototype capability and regulated production service

## Current RC status
Validated in the current RC workflow: source, policy/RBAC, consent, sanitization, provenance, automated tests, web shell, API runtime and local-first mobile boot.

Still requiring independent completion/verification before production claims: Android build, iOS signing, provider certification, device testing and final release publication.

## Regulatory note
URBAN ONE is not represented here as a bank, licensed AIS/PIS provider, payment institution or certified production financial service. Regulated capabilities must be implemented through appropriately licensed providers and validated for each target jurisdiction.

- `[DEV]` developer contribution
- `[SECURITY]` security review

- `[AI]` model/agent/data integration
