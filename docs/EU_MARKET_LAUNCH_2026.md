# URBAN ONE — EU27 B2B growth & revenue operating plan
**Prepared:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** source and public-facing offers; no paid campaign, bulk marketing or provider activation.

## Goal and decision architecture
Position URBAN ONE as an **independent B2B software/professional services** project, not a licensed payment institution, an existing Booking.com connectivity partner, or an approved OpenAI/InfoCert/Fabrick representative.

**Backward chain:** verified EUR revenue > paying external customer > signed, priced, lawful engagement > qualified inbound request > truthful landing page > demonstrated technical capability. Stop claims and expenditure at any missing prerequisite.

**Forward chain:** public five-language pages and contact > non-confidential discovery > private scoped offer > signed agreement > Stripe merchant-hosted collection as appropriate > authoritative payment confirmation > actual delivery > invoice/tax handling > refund/dispute handling and receipt ledger. No subscription, levy or bank transaction is created by these pages.

## Conversion lifecycle and acceptance
Stages: `INBOUND_UNVERIFIED` -> `QUALIFIED` -> `DISCOVERY_DONE` -> `PROPOSAL_SENT` -> `SIGNED` -> `PAYMENT_VERIFIED` -> `DELIVERED` -> `RECONCILED`; optional `LOST`, `DECLINED`, `REFUNDED`.

A status advances only with dated evidence. Track private customer details in an approved private workspace (never in public GitHub). Never count email opened, checkout link visited, simulated payment or Stripe fee movement as verified sales.

Metrics to monitor *only once measured*: qualified inbound lead count by language/sector, sales-qualified rate, proposal conversion, signed deal count, paid gross EUR, refunded EUR, Stripe fees, net receipts, support effort, contribution margin and approved provider spend. Dashboard must differentiate 0, unknown, not measured, simulated and live.

## Commercial boundaries
- One-off service fees after agreement, directly to Stripe's registered merchant account.
- Recurring SaaS only after approved SKU, pricing, cancellation, VAT treatment, terms, webhooks, paid-entitlement reconciliation and relevant mobile/app-store payment policies.
- Contracted B&B software monthly fees or verified direct booking/optional-service commissions must never attach to OTA accommodation amounts without the signed agreements and authorized payment role.
- Partner revenue share requires independently verified partners and written split terms; no implied OpenAI share.
- Enterprise licences priced per negotiated geography, IP ownership, exclusivity, escrow and support responsibilities; no promised €1m close or pipeline valuation.
- Fabrick commercial quote with long contractual duration and InfoCert pricing/eligibility need negotiation before adding recurring cost. No API service subscriptions or paid ads until unit economics and approval.
