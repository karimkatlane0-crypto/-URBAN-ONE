For eligible centres and non-profit organisations, the reference monthly value is fully waived. No CMS subscription, setup fee or basic publishing fee is charged to the centre.

Transparent terms
  • CMS access and basic editorial support: $0.
  • Default revenue share: 0%.
  • Donations and centre funds remain under the organisation's direct control; URBAN ONE does not collect or retain them.

Third-party domains, premium hosting, paid plugins, external services and provider fees are excluded and will never be activated without a separate quote and written consent. Free WordPress features are used where available, subject to the selected plan's limits.

02 / ONBOARDING

Four clear steps.

Access is created only for a named and authorised centre representative. No shared passwords.

1. Request

The centre identifies its authorised representative and website.

2. Verify

Eligibility and the requested editorial scope are confirmed.

3. Access

A named account is invited with the minimum necessary role.

4. Publish

The centre reviews and approves every publication.
